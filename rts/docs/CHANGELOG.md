- Skirmish AI v2, package C (army), spec `docs/specs/skirmish_ai_v2.md`, reviewed by the sim-reviewer:
  - Production buys one unit at a time: the composition unit with the largest deficit goes to the lowest-ID free producer and its cost is held when unaffordable. Cause of the old skew, confirmed by a failing test: producers were served in ID order and a Roost (or Tidepool) spent the bank on the only unit it can make before the Windspire (or Shell Forge) got a turn.
  - Massing units rally at the HQ, not the Core; only the group that began an attack is ordered, later units rally at home; with `rally_at_core` the group stages at the Core.
  - Defend holds for `defend_hold_seconds` after the last threat; the attack threshold is redrawn only when an attack ends.
  - Kill mode: an enemy HQ seen within `kill_seen_within_seconds` at or below `kill_hq_health_fraction` is attacked with `kill_min_army_supply` or more, without staging or retreat. Dead units are dropped from the attack group each think.
  - An extra production building is built when the bank reaches `extra_producer_bank_common` with every producer busy (up to `max_producers`).
  - Not yet done: rare-cost units in the mix, and four `PACKAGE B HOOK` places in `AiBrain.cs` (home position during a relocation, income-gated reservations, all-in definition).

- P-003 package B (movement), spec `docs/specs/p003_rules.md`, reviewed by the sim-reviewer:
  - Units on the same layer push apart instead of stacking (step 5c, new `Units/UnitSeparation.cs`): pushes come from a position snapshot with exact fixed-point sums and a uniform-grid neighbour search, so the result does not depend on visiting order or cell size.
  - A keep-right sidestep lets units pass head-on; a moving unit near its goal that touches a stopped, pushable unit nearer the goal ends its move (crowd arrival).
  - Pushes are terrain-checked. Buildings, HQs and speed-0 units are never pushed; gathering workers take no part; air units push only air units.
  - Six new keys in `tuning/terrain.json`: `separation_strength`, `sidestep_strength`, `separation_max_push_tiles_per_tick`, `crowd_arrival_radius_tiles`, `separation_cell_tiles`, `air_separation_enabled`.
  - Review fixes: a step shorter than 2^-16 tiles no longer divides by zero in the sidestep; buildings and posts no longer trigger crowd arrival.
  - Hashes changed again: headless smoke is `06ab47ea375f0430`, economy_smoke `f4e4a16805e63190`, golden hash re-pinned. The quake relocation speed-0 test runs with separation off because it stacks two units on one tile on purpose.

- P-003 package A (combat), spec `docs/specs/p003_rules.md`, reviewed by the sim-reviewer:
  - Ranged hits on higher ground deal 0.75 damage (`uphill_ranged_damage_multiplier` in `tuning/combat.json`); melee, air attackers, air targets and a shooter on a ramp are exempt.
  - Workers (Fledgling, Tender) carry a weak melee weapon (3 kinetic, 0.3 tiles, 1 s). New target picks prefer armed units; a unit never switches target on its own.
  - `hit_delay_ticks` on weapons (0 to 10; 2 on every shipped ranged weapon, 0 for melee). Damage is fixed at fire time, the hit cannot be dodged, and it waits in the hashed `PendingHitQueue`.
  - The Shellback's always-on `shield_dome` (radius 3 tiles, pool 150, recharge after 4 s at 25 per second): absorbs ranged shots from outside the dome aimed at allied units inside; a dome that went down returns only when full.
  - New events `ShotFiredEvent`, `ShieldDomeHitEvent`, `ShieldDomeDownEvent`, `ShieldDomeUpEvent`. Combat step 6 is now 6a dome regen, 6b land due hits, 6c unit loop, 6d deaths. `StatusSystem.ApplyDrenchHit` takes the attacker's ID and owner.
  - Every state hash changed: headless smoke is `59f817c2c9d3a5b4`, economy_smoke `e21eda591302b212`, golden hash re-pinned. Unity parity must be re-checked on the PC. Not yet adjusted: the skirmish AI counts workers as armed power, and Unity's selection treats workers as armed.
