# Economy (Phase 1)

Two resources (common and rare), gathered the same way by every faction from map fields that run out. Everything is deterministic: units are visited in ascending ID order, distances are compared squared in `Fix64`, and all numbers are data (`rts/data/tuning/economy.json`, `supply.json`, `hq.json` and the unit files).

- `EconomySystem.cs`: runs once per tick after combat. The header comment lists every rule: the worker loop (walk to the field, queue for one of the field's gather slots, wait `gather_seconds_per_trip`, take `gather_amount_per_trip`, walk to the nearest drop-off, deposit, repeat until the field is empty), production queues (cost paid and supply reserved when queued, the unit appears next to the producer after `build_seconds`), build orders (`build_structure` names a worker; it walks to the site, spends `worker_placement_seconds` there and the structure appears; the cost is paid when the order is accepted and refunded if the order is cancelled first), structure construction (starts at `construction_start_health_fraction`, gains health evenly, no production, drop-off or supply until complete), single-tile footprints (a `Footprint` blocker on the structure's tile while it lives) and supply.
- `EconomyRules.cs`: supply cost of a unit type (a static defence costs `static_defence_supply_cost`), build time in ticks, drop-off lookup (a complete `is_drop_off` structure or a Rooted HQ, own units only) and spawn-tile search.
- `ResourceFields.cs`, `ResourceFieldState.cs`: the map's `resource_fields` as live state, remaining amount per field, hashed in ascending field index.
- `GatherState.cs`: Idle, ToField, Waiting (queued for a slot; ties go to the fewest slot grants so far), Gathering, ToDropOff (stored on `Unit`, hashed).

Per-player stock (`Common`, `Rare`) and supply (`SupplyUsed`, `SupplyCap`) are on `Teams/Player` and are hashed. Supply used is recomputed by a scan of all units and queues (O(n)) at the end of every economy step and before each train or build command; pending build orders count as supply used; the cap is `min(supply_cap, base_supply + provides_supply of complete structures)`.

Commands live in `Commands/EconomyCommands.cs` (`gather`, `train_unit`, `cancel_production`, `set_rally_point`, `build_structure`, `build_wall_line`; their rejection reasons are listed in each class), events in `Events/EconomyEvents.cs` (`resource_deposited`, `field_depleted`, `unit_trained`, `production_cancelled`, `structure_completed`, `build_order_accepted`, `build_order_started`, `build_order_cancelled`, `build_line_tile_skipped`, `structure_placed`). The HQ rules (Rooted drop-off and worker training, Last Stand, defeat) are in `Units/HqSystem.cs`.

Placeholders and limits in Phase 1: any number of workers may share a field; nothing is refunded when a producer dies; structures are one tile; an HQ that roots does not re-check that its field still has resources. Supply limits are data in `rts/data/tuning/supply.json`.

Prerequisites (walls_and_tech.md T.1): a unit file may list `requires` (IDs of own buildings of the same faction). `train_unit` and `build_structure` check them when the order is given (`EconomyRules.FirstMissingRequirement`; rejection `missing_prerequisite`); losing a building later undoes nothing. Direct spawns ignore `requires`.

Research (research_and_unlocks.md): a building lists research items in `researches` (data in `rts/data/research`). `train_unit` with a research ID queues it in the same production queue (cost paid when queued, once per player: `already_researched`, `already_queued`); at the head it counts down `research_seconds` (`EconomyRules.QueueEntryTicks`), needs no spawn tile, and at 0 adds the ID to `Player.Researched` and emits `research_completed`. `requires` may name a research ID; `FirstMissingRequirement` then checks the owner's set. Losing the building loses the item in progress (no refund); finished research is kept. Queries: `EconomyRules.IsResearchQueued`, `QueueEntryTicks`, `Player.HasResearched`.

Wall lines (walls_and_tech.md D): `build_wall_line` takes a worker, a wall or gate definition (`is_wall`) and a tile list (Unity draws it with `WallLine.Rasterise`, the same 8-connected Bresenham line the simulation accepts, and previews each tile with `EconomyRules.WallLineTileProblem`). The command pays for every tile it keeps at once (`wall_line_max_tiles` in `tuning/economy.json` caps the list), makes the first tile the worker's build order and stores the rest in `Unit.BuildQueue` (hashed). `EconomySystem.AdvanceBuildQueue` takes the next tile when a structure is placed or the active tile is cancelled with resume; tiles that fail are skipped with `build_line_tile_skipped` and refunded, and any other order, or the worker's death, refunds the active tile and every queued tile. Each queued tile reserves its supply. D replaces the client-side list of walls_and_tech.md W.5.
